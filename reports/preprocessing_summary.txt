PREPROCESSING AND LEAKAGE-PREVENTION REPORT
===========================================
Dataset Source: CDC BRFSS 2015 Diabetes Indicators
Total Original Observations: 253680
Train Split: 202944 (80.0%)
Test Split:  50736 (20.0%)
Stratification: By Diabetes_binary class (Preserved positive ratio: ~15.76%)
Random State: 42

Feature Transformations:
  - Numerical (3 features: ['BMI', 'MentHlth', 'PhysHlth']): Median Imputation + StandardScaler
  - Ordinal (3 features: ['GenHlth', 'Education', 'Income']): Median Imputation + StandardScaler
  - Binary (13 features: ['HighBP', 'HighChol', 'CholCheck', 'Smoker', 'Stroke', 'HeartDiseaseorAttack', 'PhysActivity', 'Fruits', 'Veggies', 'HvyAlcoholConsump', 'AnyHealthcare', 'NoDocbcCost', 'DiffWalk']): Most Frequent Imputation (No scaling, preserved as 0/1 indicators)
  - Demographics (2 features: ['Sex', 'Age']): Median Imputation + StandardScaler for model inputs
  - Demographic Audit Meta (Sex_Label, Age_Group): Preserved unscaled and unaltered for subgroup fairness evaluation

Data Leakage Safeguards:
  1. All imputers and scalers fit STRICTLY on training split (X_train_raw).
  2. Test split (X_test_raw) was held out untouched until final model evaluation.
  3. Cross-validation applies pipeline transformations strictly within training folds.
