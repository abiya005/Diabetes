STATISTICAL VALIDATION PROTOCOL & LIMITATIONS
============================================
1. Paired Student's t-test:
   - Assumptions: Differences between paired fold scores are normally distributed.
   - Limitation: Overlapping training folds in repeated CV introduce mild dependence,
     which can result in slightly liberal p-values. Hence, reported alongside Wilcoxon non-parametric test.

2. Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test:
   - Non-parametric counterpart relaxing normality assumption.
   - Tests median rank difference across matched fold evaluations.

3. McNemar's Chi-Squared Test:
   - Measures whether the discordant prediction errors between two models on the test set differ significantly.
   - Edwards continuity correction (|b - c| - 1)^2 / (b + c) was applied to avoid Type I inflation.

4. Subgroup Uncertainty:
   - Smaller demographic brackets (e.g. 18-34 young adults) have higher variance in positive prevalence,
     which is accounted for via 500-sample bootstrap percentile confidence intervals.
